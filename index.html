Kontenbewokbot

Kontenbewokbot is a content-creation assistant for short-video creators. It helps prepare videos and captions, and publishes them to the creator's own TikTok account only after the creator explicitly approves each post.

How it works

  1. The creator prepares a video and caption in a private Telegram chat with the assistant.
  2. The creator connects their TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit and grants permission (user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).
  3. The assistant shows an approval screen: account name, caption, privacy level, comment/duet/stitch settings, AI-generated-content label, and commercial-content disclosure.
  4. Nothing is posted until the creator presses Approve & Post. Videos can also be sent to the TikTok inbox as a draft.

What it does not do

  • It does not post without approval.
  • It does not read or collect other users' data.
  • It does not sell or share data.
