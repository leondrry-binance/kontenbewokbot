Kontenbewokbot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains how Kontenbewokbot ("the app") handles information when a creator connects a TikTok account.

Information we access

  • TikTok account basics (display name, avatar) via user.info.basic, shown on the approval screen.
  • Posting settings available to the creator's account (for example allowed privacy levels), used to show valid options.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok after the creator logs in.
  • Videos and captions the creator chooses to publish, uploaded to TikTok only after approval.

How we use it

Only to upload or publish the creator's own videos to the creator's own TikTok account at the creator's request. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored on the operator's private device and are not published. We do not sell, rent, or share TikTok data with third parties. Videos are processed on the operator's own systems and sent directly to TikTok.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the connection is active. The creator can disconnect at any time by removing the app in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps), and can request deletion of any stored data by contacting us. Stored tokens are then deleted.

Children

The app is not intended for users under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
